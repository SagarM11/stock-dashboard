💥 Single-Stock Bet — 100% of ₹1cr in one name

Probabilities are realised historical frequencies, not forecasts. P(hit reward) = share of past cases with the SAME pattern (× volatility bucket) where price touched +reward% before −risk% within the horizon. Survivorship-inflated; "touch" ≠ you captured it; a 100%-single-stock bet carries idiosyncratic catastrophe risk (fraud / gap / halt) not in these numbers. Note P(loss) usually exceeds P(win) — the bet works only when the reward:risk payoff compensates. Not advice.
