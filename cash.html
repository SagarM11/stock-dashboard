💵 Cash Positions

Best cash positions — ranked by expected value

Read EV% honestly: EV = P(target)·(3·ATR reward) − P(stop)·(1.5·ATR risk), with P(touch) from each stock's GARCH(1,1) vol forecast over a 25-session horizon. Because the model is driftless, EV% rises with volatility — a higher-vol name is more likely to touch any barrier, so this board tilts toward the most volatile names (see the GARCH σ column). It sizes the MOVE, not the DIRECTION — direction is assumed from the momentum setup, and this is not the tested edge (that's the trailing strategy on the 🎯 Candidates / 🖥️ Desk tabs). Cross-reference RS and edge = P(t)−P(s). Not personalised advice.
