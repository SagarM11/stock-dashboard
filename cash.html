💵 Cash Positions

Best cash positions — ranked by expected value

EV = P(target)·(3·ATR reward) − P(stop)·(1.5·ATR risk), where P(touch) comes from each stock's own GARCH(1,1) vol forecast over a 25-session horizon. The vol model is driftless — it sizes the MOVE, not the DIRECTION; direction is assumed from the momentum setup. This ranks vol-adjusted reward/risk; the only backtested edge is the trailing strategy on the 🎯 Candidates / 🖥️ Desk tabs. Not personalised advice.
