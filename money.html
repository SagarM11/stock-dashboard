🎚️ Concentration scenario — "all-in on the best, then rotate?"

Backtest of hold the top-K momentum names, rotate monthly over ~7y, after costs. Absolute CAGR is survivorship-inflated (the panel is today's index members, so picking the hottest name is hindsight-cheating — and TOP-1 exploits that most) — read the shape, not the level: Sharpe is ~flat across K, while drawdown explodes as you concentrate. And this cannot see single-name catastrophe (a fraud / gap / halt on 100% of capital), which makes real all-in risk worse than shown.
